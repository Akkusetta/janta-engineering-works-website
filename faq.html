Frequently asked questions

Common questions from clients and tender officers evaluating Janta Engineering Works.

Is Janta Engineering Works a registered contractor?

Yes. JEW operates under PAN ARTPP0199H and has executed direct contracts with government Water Resources Departments and PSUs including NTPC, WAPCOS and NHDC. We're in the process of adding our GST, PWD contractor class/category and EPFO/ESIC registration details here — get in touch if you need these for a tender submission and we'll share documentation directly.

Which states and regions do you operate in?

JEW is based in Rakha, East Singhbhum, Jharkhand, and has executed projects across Jharkhand, Odisha, West Bengal and Madhya Pradesh.

What size of projects do you take on?

Current ongoing contracts range up to ₹16.64 Cr (Tenughat Dam O&M) and ₹8.30 Cr (Durgapur Barrage spillway gates). We take on both large multi-year O&M contracts and smaller fabrication/repair assignments.

Do you handle design as well as fabrication and erection?

Yes. JEW has in-house design facilities and a qualified team of engineers and draftsmen who prepare and authenticate drawings alongside fabrication, erection and commissioning.

Do you provide post-installation operation & maintenance support?

Yes — long-term O&M is a core part of our work, including an eight-year continuous gantry crane and radial gate maintenance engagement at Tenughat Dam.

What safety practices do you follow on site?

Every project begins with a site risk assessment. PPE is mandatory at all times, field crews receive hazard-recognition and emergency-response training, and incidents/near-misses are logged and investigated.
