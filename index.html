Introduction

Seventeen years in hydro-mechanical & civil works

Janta Engineering Works was established in 2008 and has since built a reputation in hydro-mechanical works, fabrication & erection, and tankage works spanning several states in India.

A qualified team of designers and engineers, in-house design facilities and skilled technical manpower let us concurrently manage assignments across diverse geographies and site conditions.
